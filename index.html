questions, answered

What is Integrate Health?

Integrate Health is an AI medical scribe built for functional, integrative and holistic medicine. It captures your visit, writes a structured SOAP note in seconds and syncs it to your EHR.

Is Integrate Health HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Integrate Health is HIPAA compliant and we sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every practice.

Does it work with my EHR?

EHR sync is included on the Pro and Practice plans, so notes go to your EHR in one click. Book a quick call and we will confirm your specific EHR.

Can it handle long, in-depth visits?

Yes. Integrate is designed for 30+ minute functional medicine visits and keeps the detail, including full histories, supplements, protocols and care plans.

Can I customize how my notes are written?

Yes. Pro and Practice include custom templates so notes match your documentation style. Essential includes a limited set of templates.

How much does it cost?

Essential is $79/mo, Pro is $129/mo and Practice is $399/mo for up to 4 providers. For a limited time, the first 100 clinics can get Pro for $79/mo for their first 12 months.
