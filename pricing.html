pricing questions

What is the founders rate?

For a limited time, the first 100 clinics can get Integrate Pro for $79/mo for their first 12 months. After that it moves to the regular Pro price of $129/mo.

Which plan is right for me?

Essential suits providers with a lighter patient load. Pro is for full-time providers and adds unlimited AI documentation, custom templates and EHR sync. Practice covers up to 4 providers and adds practice templates and admin tools.

Is there annual billing?

Yes. Annual billing is about 5% less than paying monthly. Choose it at checkout.

Is Integrate Health HIPAA compliant?

Yes. Integrate Health is HIPAA compliant and we sign a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) with every practice.

Do you offer custom automation?

Yes. Integrate Automate builds custom AI agents for intake, scheduling, follow-ups and billing, priced by quote. Book a call to scope it.
